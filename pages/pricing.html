FAQ

Pricing questions

How is GPU-hour billing calculated?

You're billed per second of active GPU time across your endpoints, rounded up to the nearest minute. Scale-to-zero periods are never billed.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle so you don't lose usage you've already paid for.

What GPU types are available?

A100 40GB and 80GB, H100, and L40S are available today on Pro and Enterprise plans. The Free tier runs on shared A100 40GB capacity.

Do you offer nonprofit or research discounts?

Yes — academic labs and registered nonprofits get 40% off Pro pricing. Reach out from the contact page with a verifying email or org link.
