Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

Overview

This policy describes what information Accelerate Labs ("Accelerate", "we", "us") collects when you use our website and platform, and how that information is used, stored, and shared. This is placeholder legal text for a template project and should be reviewed by counsel before use in production.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you contact us or create an account, and information collected automatically, such as usage data and telemetry generated by your deployed endpoints.

  • Account data: name, email, organization, billing information.
  • Usage data: API calls, endpoint configuration, resource consumption.
  • Device data: browser type, IP address, general location derived from IP.

How we use information

We use collected information to operate and improve the platform, process billing, respond to support requests, and communicate service updates. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active. Telemetry data is retained according to your plan's retention window, as described on the pricing page, after which it is automatically deleted.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us at the address below to exercise these rights.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to our contact page or privacy@accelerate.example.com.
