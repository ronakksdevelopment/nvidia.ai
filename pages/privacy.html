Privacy Policy

How this site handles your data.

Last updated September 2026.

Summary

Nemotron has no application server and does not collect or store your data on any server we operate. Everything this site keeps, including your account identity, your API key, your chosen model and theme, and your conversation history, stays in your browser's local storage on your own device.

What is stored, and where

If you sign in, the name and email you enter are saved using your browser's localStorage as a local display identity; no password or verification is required or stored, because there is no server to check one against. Your OpenRouter API key, selected model, theme preference, and conversation history are saved the same way. None of this data ever leaves your device except when it is sent directly to OpenRouter's API as part of a request you initiate, such as sending a chat message or running the connection test in Settings. If you use guest mode instead of signing in, nothing is saved at all, and everything disappears when you close the tab.

Third-party services

When you use your saved API key, requests are sent directly from your browser to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai), which routes them to the selected NVIDIA Nemotron model. OpenRouter's own privacy policy governs how they and their model providers handle that traffic. We recommend reviewing it at openrouter.ai before sending sensitive information through this interface.

Clearing your data

You can remove your saved API key, model choice, and theme at any time using the Reset settings control in Settings. Signing out clears your saved name and email. You can also delete individual conversations, or clear your browser's site data for this page to remove everything at once.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not include analytics or tracking scripts.

Changes to this policy

If this project adds a backend or server-side account system in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect it.
