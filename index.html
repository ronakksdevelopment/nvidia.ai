Why Nemotron

Built for developers who ship

Every layer of Nemotron is designed for practical deployment — from a single laptop to a full data center.

Fast inference

Optimized attention and quantization paths deliver low-latency responses on NVIDIA GPUs, from RTX workstations to H100 clusters.

Open model family

Nano, Super, and Ultra variants share one architecture, so you can prototype small and scale up without rewriting your stack.

Transparent by design

Training data lineage, evaluation results, and model cards are published alongside every release — nothing is a black box.

Hardware-aware

Built and tuned alongside NVIDIA's own inference stack, so throughput and memory footprint are optimized, not an afterthought.

Runs anywhere

Deploy through the API, self-host the open weights, or run quantized builds locally — your infrastructure, your choice.

Reasoning-tuned

Post-trained on multi-step reasoning and tool-use traces, so Nemotron plans before it answers on complex tasks.
